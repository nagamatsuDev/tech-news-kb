# In Modem, there is a possible out of bounds write due to a missing bounds check\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-20556
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **OS**: Network/Firmware
- **公開**: 2026-11-02

## 原文

In Modem, there is a possible out of bounds write due to a missing bounds check\. This could lead to remote escalation of privilege, if a UE has connected to a rogue base station controlled by the attacker, with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\. Patch ID\: MOLY01876662; Issue ID\: MSV\-9833\.
