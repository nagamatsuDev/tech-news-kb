# In LogicalLink\:\:SignalError of logical\_link\.cc, there is a possible use after free due to an error callback that can synchronously destro\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-95451
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-23

## 原文

In LogicalLink\:\:SignalError of logical\_link\.cc, there is a possible use after free due to an error callback that can synchronously destroy the link while its channels are still being iterated\. This could lead to remote \(proximal/adjacent\) code execution with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
