# A weakness has been identified in osCommerce 4\.14\.63493\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-134891
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.3)
- **言語**: PHP
- **公開**: 2026-11-23

## 原文

A weakness has been identified in osCommerce 4\.14\.63493\. This affects the function actionGiftCard of the file lib/frontend/controllers/CatalogController\.php of the component Virtual Gift Card Basket\. Executing a manipulation of the argument products\_id can lead to authorization bypass\. The attack can be executed remotely\. The vendor was contacted early about this disclosure but did not respond in any way\.
