# A vulnerability was identified in Netis WF2409E up to 20261026\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-136136
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 10.0)
- **公開**: 2026-11-25

## 原文

A vulnerability was identified in Netis WF2409E up to 20261026\. Affected by this issue is some unknown functionality of the file /cgi\-bin/skk\_set\.cgi of the component CGI Interface\. The manipulation of the argument app/password/new\_pwd\_confirm/passwd\_set/mode\_name/wl\_link leads to improper authentication\. The attack may be initiated remotely\. The exploit is publicly available and might be used\. The vendor was contacted early about this disclosure but did not respond in any way\.
