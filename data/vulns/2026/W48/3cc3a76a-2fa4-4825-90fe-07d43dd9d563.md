# In Phase2SecureConnections\:\:OnPeerPublicKey of phase\_2\_secure\_connections\.cc, there is a possible way to learn the passkey used during pa\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-95460
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-23

## 原文

In Phase2SecureConnections\:\:OnPeerPublicKey of phase\_2\_secure\_connections\.cc, there is a possible way to learn the passkey used during pairing due to an incomplete check for a reflected public key\. This could lead to remote \(proximal/adjacent\) escalation of privilege with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is needed for exploitation\.
