# In HandleRxPdu of channel\.cc, there is a possible use after free due to the channel being destroyed while it is still processing a receiv\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-95450
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-23

## 原文

In HandleRxPdu of channel\.cc, there is a possible use after free due to the channel being destroyed while it is still processing a received frame\. This could lead to remote \(proximal/adjacent\) code execution with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
