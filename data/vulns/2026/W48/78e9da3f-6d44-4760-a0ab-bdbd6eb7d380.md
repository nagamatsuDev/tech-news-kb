# Budibase from 3\.41\.0 before 4\.0\.0 contains an authentication bypass vulnerability that allows unauthenticated attackers to take over pass\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-137499
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-26

## 原文

Budibase from 3\.41\.0 before 4\.0\.0 contains an authentication bypass vulnerability that allows unauthenticated attackers to take over password\-less global Builder accounts via unverified\-email OIDC/SSO account linking\. Attackers can assert a victim's email with email\_verified\=false at a trusted IdP, bypassing the isAccountLinkable allowlist to control all apps, datasources, and automations\.
