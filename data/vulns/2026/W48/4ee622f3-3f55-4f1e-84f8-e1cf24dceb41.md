- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-23

## 原文

In EnhancedRetransmissionModeRxEngine\:\:ProcessPdu of enhanced\_retransmission\_mode\_rx\_engine\.cc, there is a possible use after free due to a connection failure callback that synchronously destroys the channel and its engines while a received frame is still being processed\. This could lead to remote \(proximal/adjacent\) code execution with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
