# llama\.cpp from b7118 before b12604 contains an integer underflow vulnerability in llama\_grammar\_parser\:\:parse\_sequence\(\) that allows unau\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-137057
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-25

## 原文

llama\.cpp from b7118 before b12604 contains an integer underflow vulnerability in llama\_grammar\_parser\:\:parse\_sequence\(\) that allows unauthenticated remote attackers to cause denial of service by supplying GBNF repetitions with max lower than min\. Attackers can send a grammar like \{5,2\} in the grammar field of a llama\-server /completion request, wrapping n\_opt to generate endless repetition rules that hang the process and exhaust memory\.
