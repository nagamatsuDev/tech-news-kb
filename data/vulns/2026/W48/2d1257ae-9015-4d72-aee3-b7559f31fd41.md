# Authorization bypass using a user\-controlled token \(IDOR\) on the GlobalEduca job portal\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-124828
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.1)
- **公開**: 2026-11-24

## 原文

Authorization bypass using a user\-controlled token \(IDOR\) on the GlobalEduca job portal\. The GET endpoint ‘/attachments/\{attachment\_id\}’ fails to verify whether the attachment belongs to the authenticated user\. A user with a registered account can enumerate sequential identifiers and download attachments belonging to other candidates, including resumes containing personal data\.
