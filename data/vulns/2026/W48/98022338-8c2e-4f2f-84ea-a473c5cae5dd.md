# In LogicalLink\:\:SignalError of logical\_link\.cc, there is a possible use after free due to the object being destroyed by a callback while \.\.\.

- **公開**: 2026-11-23

## 原文

In LogicalLink\:\:SignalError of logical\_link\.cc, there is a possible use after free due to the object being destroyed by a callback while it is still iterating over its channels\. This could lead to remote \(proximal/adjacent\) code execution with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
