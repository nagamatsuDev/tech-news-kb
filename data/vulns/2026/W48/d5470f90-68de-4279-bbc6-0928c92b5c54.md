# SRS 4\.0\.0 before 6\.0\.194, 7\.0\.0 before 7\.0\.214, and 8\.0\.0 before 8\.0\.73 contains a heap\-based buffer overflow in SrsRtcpCommon\:\:decode an\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-136626
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.2)
- **公開**: 2026-11-25

## 原文

SRS 4\.0\.0 before 6\.0\.194, 7\.0\.0 before 7\.0\.214, and 8\.0\.0 before 8\.0\.73 contains a heap\-based buffer overflow in SrsRtcpCommon\:\:decode and SrsRtcpTWCC\:\:decode that lets remote WebRTC peers write past the fixed 1500\-byte payload\_ array\. Unauthenticated attackers opening a WHIP or WHEP session can send oversized BYE, APP, unknown\-type or TWCC RTCP packets to overwrite up to 64 KB of heap memory, crashing the server\.
