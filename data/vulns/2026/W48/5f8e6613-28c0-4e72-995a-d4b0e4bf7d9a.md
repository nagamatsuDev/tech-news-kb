# libexpat through 2\.11\.0 contains a denial of service vulnerability in defineAttribute\(\) of lib/xmlparse\.c that fails to detect duplicate \.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-137992
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-27

## 原文

libexpat through 2\.11\.0 contains a denial of service vulnerability in defineAttribute\(\) of lib/xmlparse\.c that fails to detect duplicate non\-ID \#IMPLIED or \#REQUIRED attribute declarations\. Attackers can supply a crafted XML document with repeated ATTLIST declarations in an internal DTD subset to cause quadratic CPU consumption in storeAtts\(\) during parsing\.
