# EUSurvey 1\.5\.5 through 1\.6, prior to commit d5295d5, contains a missing authorization vulnerability in the POST /forms/bulkchange endpoin\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-101863
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-23

## 原文

EUSurvey 1\.5\.5 through 1\.6, prior to commit d5295d5, contains a missing authorization vulnerability in the POST /forms/bulkchange endpoint that allows any authenticated user to take over or delete other users' surveys\. SurveyController accepts arbitrary survey IDs and privilege values, and BulkExecutor applies the requested access\-right changes or deletion without checking whether the requester can manage those surveys, allowing an ordinary ROLE\_USER account to grant itself full control of another user's survey, read its draft and results, edit the form, manage its invitations, or delete it\.
