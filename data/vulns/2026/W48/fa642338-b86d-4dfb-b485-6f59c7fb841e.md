# In StartDescriptorDiscovery of remote\_service\.cc, there is a possible use after free due to the service object being destroyed from withi\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-95425
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-23

## 原文

In StartDescriptorDiscovery of remote\_service\.cc, there is a possible use after free due to the service object being destroyed from within a synchronous callback while it is still iterating over its characteristics\. This could lead to remote \(proximal/adjacent\) code execution with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
