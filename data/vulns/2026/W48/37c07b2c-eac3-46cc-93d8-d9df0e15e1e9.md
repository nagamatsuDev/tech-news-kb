# SRS 4\.0\.0 before 6\.0\.194, 7\.0\.0 before 7\.0\.214, and 8\.0\.0 before 8\.0\.73 contains a reachable assertion in RTCP decoding that lets remote \.\.\.

- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-25

## 原文

SRS 4\.0\.0 before 6\.0\.194, 7\.0\.0 before 7\.0\.214, and 8\.0\.0 before 8\.0\.73 contains a reachable assertion in RTCP decoding that lets remote WebRTC peers crash the server\. Attackers opening an unauthenticated WHIP or WHEP session can send an 8\-byte RTCP packet with undersized length, triggering srs\_assert aborts that stop every stream\.
