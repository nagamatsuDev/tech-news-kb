# In BluetoothSetupImpl\:\:OnAudioSourceConnectChanged of bluetooth\_setup\_impl\.cc, there is a possible way for an unauthenticated nearby Blue\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-95458
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-23

## 原文

In BluetoothSetupImpl\:\:OnAudioSourceConnectChanged of bluetooth\_setup\_impl\.cc, there is a possible way for an unauthenticated nearby Bluetooth device to be saved as the audio output device due to a logic error in the code\. This could lead to remote \(proximal/adjacent\) escalation of privilege with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
