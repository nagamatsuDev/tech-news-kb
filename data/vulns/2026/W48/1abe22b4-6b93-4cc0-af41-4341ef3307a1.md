# In OnEncryptionChange of security\_manager\.cc, there is a possible way to establish a bond marked as authenticated without completing auth\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-95420
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-23

## 原文

In OnEncryptionChange of security\_manager\.cc, there is a possible way to establish a bond marked as authenticated without completing authenticated pairing due to a logic error in the code\. This could lead to remote \(proximal/adjacent\) escalation of privilege with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
