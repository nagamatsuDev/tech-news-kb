# svglib before 2\.5\.0 contains a denial of service vulnerability in SvgRenderer\.renderNode\(\) that recursively renders internal &lt;image&gt; href\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-137952
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-27

## 原文

svglib before 2\.5\.0 contains a denial of service vulnerability in SvgRenderer\.renderNode\(\) that recursively renders internal &lt;image&gt; href fragment references without expansion limits or cycle detection\. Attackers can supply crafted SVGs to svg2rlg\(\) with nested groups referencing each other twice or self\-referencing images to cause exponential resource consumption or RecursionError crashes\.
