# A flaw was found in json\-glib\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-120194
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-10-31

## 原文

A flaw was found in json\-glib\. The JSONPath slice evaluator used by json\_path\_query\(\) can enter an infinite loop when given a slice expression with a negative step\. An application that evaluates attacker\-controlled JSONPath expressions can be forced into a hang, leading to a denial of service\.
