# System service 'antaiuiserver' exposes transaction 14 \(sendMyATCmd\) &amp; 4, allowing ANY app on device to send arbitrary AT commands to mode\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-128796
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-24

## 原文

System service 'antaiuiserver' exposes transaction 14 \(sendMyATCmd\) &amp; 4, allowing ANY app on device to send arbitrary AT commands to modem without permissions \(no READ\_PHONE\_STATE, SEND\_SMS\)\. Enables reading IMEI, IMSI, ICCID, and managing SMS\.
