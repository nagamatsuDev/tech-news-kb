# FFmpeg through 9\.0\.2 contains a heap buffer overflow vulnerability in aribcaption\_trans\_text\_subtitle\(\) in libavcodec/libaribcaption\.c wh\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-125210
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.0)
- **公開**: 2026-11-09

## 原文

FFmpeg through 9\.0\.2 contains a heap buffer overflow vulnerability in aribcaption\_trans\_text\_subtitle\(\) in libavcodec/libaribcaption\.c when decoding with sub\_type text\. Attackers can supply a crafted ARIB STD\-B24 caption stream with zero regions, causing a pointer write past a zero\-sized sub\-&gt;rects allocation and corrupting heap memory\.
