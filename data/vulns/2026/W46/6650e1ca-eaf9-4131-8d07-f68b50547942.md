- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-128085 / GHSA\-q7gw\-24fr\-5p69
- **危険度**: Medium（警告） (CVSS 5.3)
- **言語**: Go
- **パッケージ**: Go/go\.opentelemetry\.io/otel/sdk &gt;\= 1\.33\.0, &lt; 1\.47\.0 → 1\.47\.0, Go/go\.opentelemetry\.io/otel/sdk/log &gt;\= 0\.9\.0, &lt; 1\.47\.0 → 1\.47\.0, Go/go\.opentelemetry\.io/otel/trace &gt;\= 1\.35\.0, &lt; 1\.47\.0 → 1\.47\.0
- **公開**: 2026-11-13

## 原文

\#\# Summary The \`AttributeValueLengthLimit\` span/log\-record limit can be bypassed entirely with attribute values that consist of valid U\+FFFD \(REPLACEMENT CHARACTER\) runes\. The counting\-based truncation introduced by the fix for GHSA\-p9f8\-wvj8\-2fg8 \(commit \`e016a78c9\`, first released in v1\.33\.0\) has an "optimistic" fast path that skips every U\+FFFD without counting it and without falling back to the correct slow path\.
