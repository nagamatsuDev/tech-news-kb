# In aes256\_cmac\_sw of aes\.rs, there is a possible way to forge message authentication codes over multi\-block messages due to improperly us\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-56898
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.4)
- **公開**: 2026-11-10

## 原文

In aes256\_cmac\_sw of aes\.rs, there is a possible way to forge message authentication codes over multi\-block messages due to improperly used crypto\. This could lead to local escalation of privilege with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
