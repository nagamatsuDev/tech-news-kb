# Soup Sieve\: Polynomial\-time denial of service in the descendant combinator

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-126190 / GHSA\-mmhr\-3c5m\-f64f
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **言語**: Python
- **パッケージ**: PyPI/soupsieve &lt; 4\.0\.0 → 4\.0\.0
- **公開**: 2026-11-12

## 原文

\#\# Summary Matching a selector that uses the descendant combinator \(\`a b\`\) takes time that grows polynomially with how deeply the document is nested, and each extra descendant combinator in the selector raises the degree by one\. A selector with \`k\` descendant combinators takes \`O\(n\^\(k\+1\)\)\` time on a chain of \`n\` nested elements\. An attacker who controls the HTML or XML that an application passes to Soup Sieve \(directly, or through Beautiful Soup's \`select\(\)\`, \`select\_one\(\)\`, \`css\.select\(\)\`, etc\.\) can send a very small, deeply nested document that keeps a CPU busy for seconds to minutes against
