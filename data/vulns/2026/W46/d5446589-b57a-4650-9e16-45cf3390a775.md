# FFmpeg through 9\.0\.2 contains a heap buffer overflow vulnerability in pcm\_bluray\_decode\_frame\(\) in libavcodec/pcm\-bluray\.c on big\-endian \.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-125214
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.0)
- **公開**: 2026-11-09

## 原文

FFmpeg through 9\.0\.2 contains a heap buffer overflow vulnerability in pcm\_bluray\_decode\_frame\(\) in libavcodec/pcm\-bluray\.c on big\-endian hosts\. Attackers can supply a crafted Blu\-ray \(M2TS\) LPCM stream whose payload length is not a multiple of the sample size, writing attacker\-controlled bytes past the audio frame buffer\.
