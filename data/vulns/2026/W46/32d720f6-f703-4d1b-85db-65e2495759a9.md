# OpsTree BuildPiper before 2\.7\.0 lacks rate limiting on password\-reset OTP verification, allowing unauthenticated attackers to brute force\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-127079
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.4)
- **公開**: 2026-11-13

## 原文

OpsTree BuildPiper before 2\.7\.0 lacks rate limiting on password\-reset OTP verification, allowing unauthenticated attackers to brute force 6\-digit OTPs\. Attackers can initiate a reset and submit unthrottled guesses to the check\_otp endpoint, resetting the target's password and taking over the account\.
