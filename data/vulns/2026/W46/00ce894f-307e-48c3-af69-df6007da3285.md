# NLTK\: Symlink escape in YCOECorpusReader lets a psd/pos directory symlink read files outside the corpus root within the data sandbox

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-126304 / GHSA\-qg35\-44qx\-7vrr
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **言語**: Python
- **パッケージ**: PyPI/nltk &gt;\= 3\.10\.0, &lt;\= 3\.10\.3 → 3\.10\.4
- **公開**: 2026-11-13

## 原文

\#\# Summary \`YCOECorpusReader\` builds two nested corpus sub\-readers whose roots are derived from \`self\.root\.join\("psd"\)\` and \`self\.root\.join\("pos"\)\` \(nltk/corpus/reader/ycoe\.py\)\. Because \`FileSystemPathPointer\.join\(\)\` \(nltk/data\.py\) performs a purely lexical containment check and never resolves symlinks, and because \`CorpusReader\.\_\_init\_\_\` \(nltk/corpus/reader/api\.py\) validates a sub\-reader root only against the global NLTK data sandbox and not against the enclosing corpus root, a directory symlink named \`psd\` or \`pos\` planted inside a YCOE corpus lets the reader anchor its nested sub\-readers ou
