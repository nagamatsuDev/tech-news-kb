# The AUTH\_GSSAPI handler in lib/rpc/svc\_auth\_gssapi\.c in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) 1\.5\.2 and later allows remote attackers to cause a deni\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-112323
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-09

## 原文

The AUTH\_GSSAPI handler in lib/rpc/svc\_auth\_gssapi\.c in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) 1\.5\.2 and later allows remote attackers to cause a denial of service \(memory exhaustion in kadmind\) by sending repeated failed AUTH\_GSSAPI\_INIT requests\. Each failed request allocates client state that is only freed after a 15\-minute expiration timeout, allowing an attacker to accumulate memory faster than it is reclaimed\.
