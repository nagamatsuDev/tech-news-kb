# Dell Display and Peripheral Manager \(DDPM Windows\), versions prior to 2\.4\.0\.17, contain a Time\-of\-check Time\-of\-use \(TOCTOU\) Condition vu\.\.\.

- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.0)

## 原文
