# SvelteKit\: Build\-time server\-only module import guard could fail, resulting in server secrets being statically replaced in client bundle

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-128118 / GHSA\-m9j8\-jcmv\-hp69
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **言語**: JavaScript/TypeScript
- **パッケージ**: npm/@sveltejs/kit &gt;\= 3\.0\.0, &lt;\= 3\.1\.0 → 3\.1\.1
- **公開**: 2026-11-13

## 原文

Under certain circumstances, server\-only imports, which typically cannot be included in the client bundle, could make their way into it, potentially exposing secrets\. This could only occur when those server\-only imports were made through \`src/params\.js\`\.
