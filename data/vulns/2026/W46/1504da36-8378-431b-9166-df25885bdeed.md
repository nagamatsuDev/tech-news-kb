- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-128079 / GHSA\-jv6m\-48w5\-5rpr
- **危険度**: Medium（警告）
- **言語**: Python
- **パッケージ**: PyPI/envd &gt;\= 0\.3\.38, &lt; 1\.3\.6 → 1\.3\.6
- **公開**: 2026-11-13

## 原文

\#\#\# Summary envd’s \`install\.python\_packages\(requirements\=\.\.\.\)\` could read files outside the build context on the host\. Content interpreted as dependency entries could appear in build logs and build\-step metadata\. Commit f576c92, released in v1\.3\.5, added path containment validation but left a time\-of\-check/time\-of\-use \(TOCTOU\) race between symlink resolution and opening the requirements file\.
