# OpenEMR before 8\.5\.0 contains a missing authorization vulnerability in immunizations\.php, diagnosis\_full\.php, and billing\_process\.php tha\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-126449
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.1)
- **言語**: PHP
- **公開**: 2026-11-13

## 原文

OpenEMR before 8\.5\.0 contains a missing authorization vulnerability in immunizations\.php, diagnosis\_full\.php, and billing\_process\.php that lets low\-privileged users bypass ACL checks\. Authenticated Front Office users can reuse their session CSRF token to add or delete immunizations, write encounter billing codes, and invoke the billing processor\.
