# clnttcp\_call\(\) in lib/rpc/clnt\_tcp\.c in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) does not free the RPC reply verifier between retries, which allows remo\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-112314
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-09

## 原文

clnttcp\_call\(\) in lib/rpc/clnt\_tcp\.c in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) does not free the RPC reply verifier between retries, which allows remote attackers to cause a denial of service \(memory leak in GSS\-RPC client programs\) by injecting RPC replies with mismatched XIDs or by triggering authentication retries\.
