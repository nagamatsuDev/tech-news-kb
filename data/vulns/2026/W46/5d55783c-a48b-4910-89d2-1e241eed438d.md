## 原文

\#\# Summary \`Rails\:\:HTML\:\:PermitScrubber\` restricts the \`href\` and \`xlink\:href\` attributes of SVG reference elements to local references\. That check matched the attribute value against \`/\^\\s\*\[\^\#\\s\]\.\*/m\`\.
