# In check\_constraint\_monotonicity of lib\.rs, there is a possible way to downgrade stored security policies and bypass rollback protection \.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-59630
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.4)
- **公開**: 2026-11-10

## 原文

In check\_constraint\_monotonicity of lib\.rs, there is a possible way to downgrade stored security policies and bypass rollback protection due to a logic error\. This could lead to local escalation of privilege with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
