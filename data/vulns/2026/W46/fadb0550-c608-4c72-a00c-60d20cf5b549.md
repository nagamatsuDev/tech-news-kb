# In secure\_transport\_usecase\_handshake of secure\_transport\.c, there is a possible way to bypass secure transport channel encryption due to\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-55360
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.4)
- **公開**: 2026-11-10

## 原文

In secure\_transport\_usecase\_handshake of secure\_transport\.c, there is a possible way to bypass secure transport channel encryption due to a logic error\. This could lead to local escalation of privilege with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
