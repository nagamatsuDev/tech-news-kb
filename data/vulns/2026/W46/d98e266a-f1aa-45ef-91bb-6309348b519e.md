# decode\_sequence\_of\(\) in lib/krb5/asn\.1/asn1\_encode\.c in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) 1\.11 and later grows the decoded element array one entr\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-119079
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-09

## 原文

decode\_sequence\_of\(\) in lib/krb5/asn\.1/asn1\_encode\.c in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) 1\.11 and later grows the decoded element array one entry at a time, which allows remote attackers to cause a denial of service \(CPU consumption in the KDC\) by sending an unauthenticated AS\-REQ or TGS\-REQ with a large number of elements in a SEQUENCE OF field over TCP\.
