# Addressable\: Regular Expression Denial of Service in templates with certain exploded expressions

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-126350 / GHSA\-j82w\-28jv\-3pmj
- **危険度**: Medium（警告） (CVSS 5.9)
- **言語**: Ruby
- **パッケージ**: RubyGems/addressable &gt;\= 2\.3\.0, &lt; 2\.9\.1 → 2\.9\.1
- **公開**: 2026-11-12

## 原文

\#\#\# Impact Within the URI template implementation in Addressable, templates using the \`\*\` \(explode\) modifier with the \`/\`, \`\.\`, \`;\`, \`?\` or \`&amp;\` operators \(e\.g\., \`\{/segments\*\}\`, \`\{\.ext\*\}\`, \`\{;params\*\}\`, \`\{?query\*\}\`\) generate regular expressions vulnerable to catastrophic backtracking\. When \`Template\#match\` or \`Template\#extract\` is called with a maliciously crafted URI that starts with the operator's leading character, matching takes exponential time, leading to uncontrolled resource consumption and denial of service\. An input under 60 bytes is enough to occupy a thread for minutes\. GHSA\-h27x\-rf
