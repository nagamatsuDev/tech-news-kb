# clean\_client\(\) in lib/rpc/svc\_auth\_gssapi\.c in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) restarts its scan from the head of the client list after each re\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-112322
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-09

## 原文

clean\_client\(\) in lib/rpc/svc\_auth\_gssapi\.c in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) restarts its scan from the head of the client list after each removal, resulting in quadratic CPU cost\. Remote attackers can cause a denial of service \(CPU consumption in kadmind\) by accumulating a large number of AUTH\_GSSAPI client entries\.
