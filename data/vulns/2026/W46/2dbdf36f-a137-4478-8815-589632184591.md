## 原文

\#\# Summary \`TraceContextTextMapPropagator\.extract\(\)\` in \`opentelemetry\-api\` is vulnerable to regular expression denial of service \(ReDoS\) when processing a specially crafted \`traceparent\` value\. A malformed value containing a trace\-context prefix followed by a long whitespace sequence and an embedded newline triggers quadratic\-time regex backtracking\.
