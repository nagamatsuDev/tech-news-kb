# The GSS\-RPC transport layer in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) 1\.4 and later does not release the previous authentication context when a client\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-112320
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-09

## 原文

The GSS\-RPC transport layer in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) 1\.4 and later does not release the previous authentication context when a client switches from an RPCSEC\_GSS or AUTH\_GSSAPI flavor to a non\-GSS flavor on the same TCP connection\. This allows remote attackers to cause a denial of service \(memory exhaustion in kadmind\) by repeatedly establishing and abandoning GSS authentication contexts\.
