# OpenEMR before 8\.5\.0 contains multiple missing authorization and SQL injection defects across report, portal, transaction, and immunizati\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-126455
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.1)
- **公開**: 2026-11-13

## 原文

OpenEMR before 8\.5\.0 contains multiple missing authorization and SQL injection defects across report, portal, transaction, and immunization components\. Low\-privileged Front Office clerks can chain these to read arbitrary database tables including users\_secure hashes, hijack patient portal identities, and forge approved clinical records\.
