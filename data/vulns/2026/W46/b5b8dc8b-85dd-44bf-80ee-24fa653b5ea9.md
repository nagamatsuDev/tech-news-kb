# Frappe india\-payroll before 16\.0\.4 contains a missing authorization vulnerability in the get\_employee\_details, compute\_tax\_comparison and\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-129429
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.1)
- **公開**: 2026-11-14

## 原文

Frappe india\-payroll before 16\.0\.4 contains a missing authorization vulnerability in the get\_employee\_details, compute\_tax\_comparison and set\_tax\_regime methods of tax\_regime\_selector\.py that allows authenticated low\-privileged users to access other employees' payroll data\. Attackers can supply another employee identifier to read base pay and earning and deduction components, or change the tax regime on draft Salary Structure Assignments to affect tax withholding\.
