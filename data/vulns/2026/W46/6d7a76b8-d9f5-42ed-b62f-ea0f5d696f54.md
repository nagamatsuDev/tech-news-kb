# In chppWifiRangingEventNotification of clients/wifi\.c, there is a possible Heap Buffer Overflow due to a logic error in the code\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-58787
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-10

## 原文
