# FFmpeg through 9\.0\.2 contains a heap buffer overflow in the GIF encoder's gif\_image\_write\_image\(\) in libavcodec/gif\.c because shrunk\_buf \.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-125250
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-09

## 原文

FFmpeg through 9\.0\.2 contains a heap buffer overflow in the GIF encoder's gif\_image\_write\_image\(\) in libavcodec/gif\.c because shrunk\_buf is sized from the first frame's linesize\. Attackers can supply PAL8 video whose frame linesize grows mid\-stream, causing remap\_frame\_to\_palette\(\) to write past shrunk\_buf and corrupt the heap during GIF transcoding\.
