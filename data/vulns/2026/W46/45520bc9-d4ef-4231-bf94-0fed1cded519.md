# AutoMapper is a convention\-based object\-object mapper in \.NET\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-128081 / GHSA\-gpwp\-5797\-mjff
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **言語**: C\#/\.NET
- **公開**: 2026-11-13

## 原文

AutoMapper is a convention\-based object\-object mapper in \.NET\. Prior to 15\.1\.4 and from 16\.0\.0 until 16\.3\.0, AutoMapper can recurse without an effective MaxDepth limit when mapping polymorphic graphs through Include or IncludeBase, runtime\-typed object or interface members, nested Dictionary&lt;string, object&gt; or ExpandoObject sources, or recursive ConvertUsing type converters\. Deep attacker\-influenced graphs on these paths can throw an uncatchable StackOverflowException and terminate the process\. This issue is fixed in versions 15\.1\.4 and 16\.3\.0\.
