# OpenEMR before 8\.5\.0 lacks rate limiting or lockout on TOTP submission, allowing attackers with a valid password to brute\-force the 6\-dig\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-126454
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.1)
- **言語**: PHP
- **公開**: 2026-11-13

## 原文

OpenEMR before 8\.5\.0 lacks rate limiting or lockout on TOTP submission, allowing attackers with a valid password to brute\-force the 6\-digit MFA code\. Attackers can script repeated POST requests to main\_screen\.php or the OAuth2 authorization flow with re\-submitted hidden credentials to bypass two\-factor authentication\.
