# A improper neutralization of special elements used in a template engine vulnerability in Fortinet FortiManager 8\.0\.0 through 8\.0\.1, Forti\.\.\.

- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.4)
- **公開**: 2026-11-11
