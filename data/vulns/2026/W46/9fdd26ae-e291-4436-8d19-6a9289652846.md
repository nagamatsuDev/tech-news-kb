## 原文

\#\# Summary OpenTelemetry Go eagerly materializes W3C baggage metadata into property objects during parsing\. Slice growth, serialization, and copying into the internal representation cause \*\*bounded but disproportionate memory\-allocation amplification\*\* for a valid, densely populated header\. In v1\.46\.0 and v1\.47\.0\-rc\.1, the 8,192\-byte raw\-input limit is checked before member parsing; this is not unlimited allocation or a bypass of that limit\.
