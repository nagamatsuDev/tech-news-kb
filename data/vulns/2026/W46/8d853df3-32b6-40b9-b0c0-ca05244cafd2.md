# A use of hard\-coded credentials vulnerability in Fortinet FortiManager 8\.0\.0 through 8\.0\.1, FortiManager 7\.6\.0 through 7\.6\.7, FortiManage\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-126181
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.0)
- **公開**: 2026-11-11

## 原文

A use of hard\-coded credentials vulnerability in Fortinet FortiManager 8\.0\.0 through 8\.0\.1, FortiManager 7\.6\.0 through 7\.6\.7, FortiManager 7\.4\.0 through 7\.4\.11 may allow an adjacent\-network authenticated attacker with user permission to join the FGFM configuration\-sync mesh of the affected FortiGate deployment via the default pre\-shared key\.
