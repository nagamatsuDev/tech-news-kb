# In BitmapImageBuffer of bitmap\_image\_buffer\.h, there is a possible out\-of\-bounds write due to an integer overflow\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-58740
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-10

## 原文

In BitmapImageBuffer of bitmap\_image\_buffer\.h, there is a possible out\-of\-bounds write due to an integer overflow\. This could lead to local escalation of privilege with User execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
