# WWBN AVideo through 29\.7\.1 contains an improper payment verification vulnerability in plugin/PayPalYPT/ipnV2\.php that credits wallets fro\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-126543
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **言語**: PHP
- **公開**: 2026-11-11

## 原文

WWBN AVideo through 29\.7\.1 contains an improper payment verification vulnerability in plugin/PayPalYPT/ipnV2\.php that credits wallets from PayPal IPNs not bound to the site's merchant account\. Unauthenticated attackers can forward genuine VERIFIED recurring\-payment IPNs from their own PayPal account with a chosen users\_id to add wallet balance each billing cycle\.
