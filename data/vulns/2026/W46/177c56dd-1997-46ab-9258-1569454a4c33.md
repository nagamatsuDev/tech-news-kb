# FFmpeg 7\.1 through 9\.0\.2 contains an integer overflow in ff\_vk\_decode\_add\_slice\(\) in libavcodec/vulkan\_decode\.c that miscalculates the Vu\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-125249
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.0)
- **公開**: 2026-11-09

## 原文

FFmpeg 7\.1 through 9\.0\.2 contains an integer overflow in ff\_vk\_decode\_add\_slice\(\) in libavcodec/vulkan\_decode\.c that miscalculates the Vulkan hwaccel bitstream buffer size\. Attackers can supply a crafted stream with over 2 GiB of slice data per frame, causing memcpy\(\) to write past a smaller pooled host\-mapped buffer\.
