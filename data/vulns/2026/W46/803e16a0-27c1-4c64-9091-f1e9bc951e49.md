# The AUTH\_GSSAPI handler in lib/rpc/svc\_auth\_gssapi\.c in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) does not free decoded arguments on certain error paths,\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-112321
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-09

## 原文

The AUTH\_GSSAPI handler in lib/rpc/svc\_auth\_gssapi\.c in MIT Kerberos 5 \(aka krb5\) does not free decoded arguments on certain error paths, which allows remote attackers to cause a denial of service \(memory exhaustion in kadmind\) via crafted AUTH\_GSSAPI\_INIT requests that trigger authentication failures\.
