# In PresenceDecoderV1\:\:DecodeDataElements of presence\_decoder\_v1\.cc, there is a possible out\-of\-bounds write due to a missing bounds check\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-57004
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.4)
- **公開**: 2026-11-10

## 原文

In PresenceDecoderV1\:\:DecodeDataElements of presence\_decoder\_v1\.cc, there is a possible out\-of\-bounds write due to a missing bounds check\. This could lead to remote escalation of privilege with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\.
