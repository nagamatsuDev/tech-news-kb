# A flaw has been found in TRENDnet TEW\-657BRM 1\.00\.1\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-133985
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-21

## 原文

A flaw has been found in TRENDnet TEW\-657BRM 1\.00\.1\. Affected by this issue is some unknown functionality of the file /usr/sbin/restore\_config\.cgi\. Executing a manipulation can lead to out\-of\-bounds read\. The attack may be launched remotely\. The exploit has been published and may be used\. The vendor explains\: "I am not able to confirm if such vulnerability exists as the product has been EOL for more than 15 years\. We will send out an announcement letting customers know\." This vulnerability only affects products that are no longer supported by the maintainer\.
