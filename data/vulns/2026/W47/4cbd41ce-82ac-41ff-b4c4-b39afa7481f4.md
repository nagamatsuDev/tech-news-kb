# SDO\_downloadsegment is called whenever a mailbox frame carries the DOWNLOADSEGREQ command byte, regardless of whether a segmented downloa\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-129797
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-21

## 原文

SDO\_downloadsegment is called whenever a mailbox frame carries the DOWNLOADSEGREQ command byte, regardless of whether a segmented download is actually in progress\. ESCvar\.data, which the handler writes through, is NULL until a segmented transfer is started\. A master can send a DOWNLOADSEGREQ at any time and write through a NULL or stale pointer\.
