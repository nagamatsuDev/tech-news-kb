# The Video Conferencing with Zoom WordPress plugin before 4\.7\.0 does not check that the requester is authorized to join a meeting before i\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-114434
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **言語**: PHP
- **公開**: 2026-11-21

## 原文

The Video Conferencing with Zoom WordPress plugin before 4\.7\.0 does not check that the requester is authorized to join a meeting before issuing a Zoom Meeting SDK signature for it, allowing unauthenticated users to obtain a valid signed join credential for an arbitrary meeting\.
