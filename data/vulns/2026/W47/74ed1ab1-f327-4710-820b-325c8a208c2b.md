# IBM Guardium Data Protection 12\.2 is vulnerable to a stack\-based buffer overflow when processing a specially crafted PROXY protocol v1 he\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-82342
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.1)
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文
