- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-129229 / GHSA\-32p6\-cwhc\-8r78
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **言語**: Python
- **パッケージ**: PyPI/nltk &gt;\= 3\.10\.0, &lt;\= 3\.10\.3 → 3\.10\.4
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

\#\# Summary NLTK exposes a compile\-time regular\-expression denial of service in its regex\-search helpers\. When an application passes untrusted text as the search pattern to \`TokenSearcher\.findall\(\)\` / \`Text\.findall\(\)\`, or as the tokenizer pattern to \`RegexpTokenizer\`, the pattern is handed to the third\-party \`regex\` engine, whose compilation cost is roughly quadratic in the number of capturing groups\. A short pattern of the form \`\(\)\` repeated many times \(for example \`"\(\)" \* 25000\`, about 50 KB\) forces tens of seconds of synchronous CPU work before any match begins\.
