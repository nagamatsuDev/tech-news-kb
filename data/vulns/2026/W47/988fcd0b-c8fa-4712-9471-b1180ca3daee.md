## 原文

\#\#\# Impact \`amqtt\` is vulnerable to a remote denial of service caused by improper MQTT topic\-filter validation combined with catastrophic regular\-expression backtracking\. A single client can freeze the entire broker by sending one specially crafted \`SUBSCRIBE\` packet followed by one \`PUBLISH\` packet\. The vulnerability is caused by two defects\: 1\. \`add\_subscription\(\)\` validates the placement of the single\-level wildcard \`\+\` using substring checks for \`/\+\` and \`\+/\`, rather than verifying that each \`/\`\-delimited level containing \`\+\` is exactly \`\+\`\.
