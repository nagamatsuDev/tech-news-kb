- **ID**: GHSA\-4rj4\-rcrq\-gr92
- **危険度**: Medium（警告）
- **言語**: JavaScript/TypeScript
- **パッケージ**: npm/safari\-mcp &lt; 2\.22\.15 → 2\.22\.15
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

\#\#\# Impact Sixteen places in \`safari\.js\` placed a tool argument inside a single\-quoted JavaScript string literal and escaped the single quote without first escaping backslashes\.
