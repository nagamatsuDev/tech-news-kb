# Budibase\: Cross\-privilege Stored XSS in the Budibase Builder via broken HTML\-escaping in \`jsonFormatter\.format\(\)\` rendered through \`\{@html\}\` in the CSP\-less Builder origin

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-122244 / GHSA\-r2q3\-7m4h\-63qg
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.7)
- **言語**: JavaScript/TypeScript
- **パッケージ**: npm/@budibase/server &lt;\= 3\.38\.1
- **公開**: 2026-11-20

## 原文

\#\# Description \#\#\# Summary A \*\*low\-privileged application end\-user\*\* \(role \`BASIC\` \- \*\*not\*\* a builder or admin, and on a public data\-collection form, an unauthenticated visitor\) can store an HTML/JavaScript payload in ordinary row data\.
