# NLTK\: AllowlistUnpickler does not deny numpy submodule\-local file\-I/O sinks

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-128136 / GHSA\-c59x\-vh6j\-3w8v
- **危険度**: Medium（警告） (CVSS 6.1)
- **言語**: Python
- **パッケージ**: PyPI/nltk &gt;\= 3\.10\.0, &lt; 3\.10\.4 → 3\.10\.4
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

\#\#\# Summary \`nltk\.picklesec\.allowlisted\_pickle\_load\` keyed its denylist of dangerous globals by export name, so numpy's file\-I/O callables that live in submodules and are not re\-exported under \`\_\_module\_\_ \=\= "numpy"\` were never matched\. Under the broad \`allowed\_modules\=\("numpy", "scipy", "sklearn"\)\` allowance that \`TransitionParser\.parse\(depgraphs, modelFile\)\` granted for model files, a crafted protocol 4 pickle could name \`numpy\.lib\.\_npyio\_impl\.recfromtxt\` \(arbitrary file read, result returned to the caller of \`allowlisted\_pickle\_load\`\) or \`numpy\.lib\.format\.open\_memmap\` \(attacker\-chosen file
