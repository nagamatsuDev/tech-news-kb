# Allocation of resources without limits or throttling vulnerability in Apache Tomcat during HTTP upgrade in some circumstances lead to pos\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-118233
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

Allocation of resources without limits or throttling vulnerability in Apache Tomcat during HTTP upgrade in some circumstances lead to possible DoS\.
  
This issue affects Apache Tomcat\: from 11\.0\.0\-M1 through 11\.0\.27, from 10\.1\.0\-M1 through 10\.1\.61, from 9\.0\.41 through 9\.0\.123\.
  
The following versions were EOL at the time the CVE was created but are known to be affected\: from 8\.5\.61 through 8\.5\.100\. Other unsupported versions may also be affected\.
  
Users are recommended to upgrade to version 11\.0\.29, 10\.1\.62 or 9\.0\.124, which fix the issue\.  
Note\: Tomcat 11\.0\.28 was not released
