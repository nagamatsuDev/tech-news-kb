# In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved\: mm/khugepaged\: flush deferred unmaps before dropping a failed folio \.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-99707
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.8)
- **OS**: Linux
- **公開**: 2026-11-20

## 原文

In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved\:
  
mm/khugepaged\: flush deferred unmaps before dropping a failed folio
  
collapse\_file\(\) can fail its reference\-count or dirty\-folio check after  
unmapping with TTU\_BATCH\_FLUSH\.  Both paths put back the isolated folio,  
then unlock it and drop the lookup reference before reaching the common  
try\_to\_unmap\_flush\(\)\.
  
The page\-cache reference does not keep the folio stable once the lock is  
released\.  Another collapse can replace and free it\.  With its PTEs  
already gone, that collapse cannot flush the first task's per\-task TLB  
batch, and retract\_page\_tables\(\) skips short or unaligned VMAs\.  A CPU can  
therefore retain a user translation to the freed folio\.  This has been  
reproduced with unprivileged MADV\_COLLAPSE on a memfd\.
  
Flush at out\_unlock while the lookup reference and folio lock are still  
held\.  The common flush continues to cover the accumulated pagelist on  
both success and rollback, preserving batching on successful collapses\.
