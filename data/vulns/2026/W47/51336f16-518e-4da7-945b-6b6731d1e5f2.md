# Allocation of resources without limits or throttling vulnerability in Apache Tomcat in the CGI Servlet when handling POST bodies can resu\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-118436
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

Allocation of resources without limits or throttling vulnerability in Apache Tomcat in the CGI Servlet when handling POST bodies can result in a DoS for some inputs\.
  
This issue affects Apache Tomcat\: from 11\.0\.0\-M1 through 11\.0\.27, from 10\.1\.0\-M1 through 10\.1\.61, from 9\.0\.0\.M1 through 9\.0\.123\.
  
The following versions were EOL at the time the CVE was created but are known to be affected\: from 8\.5\.0 through 8\.5\.100, from 7\.0\.0 through 7\.0\.109\. Other unsupported versions may also be affected\.
  
Users are recommended to upgrade to version 11\.0\.29, 10\.1\.62 or 9\.0\.124, which fix the issue\.  
Note\: Tomcat 11\.0\.28 was not released
