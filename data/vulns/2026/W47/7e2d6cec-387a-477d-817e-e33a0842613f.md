## 原文

\#\#\# Summary The \`auth/aws\` package of the \`chainguard\.dev/sdk\` Go library provides Chainguard's AWS\-backed identity federation \(an AWS workload proves its identity to Chainguard by signing an AWS STS \`GetCallerIdentity\` request\)\. Its verifier, \`VerifyToken\`, reads the \`Chainguard\-Audience\` and \`Chainguard\-Identity\` binding headers from a presented token but does not require them to be covered by the SigV4 signature\.
