# In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved\: drm/panthor\: Fix TOCTOU race between GROUP\_REGISTERED check and eras\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-99480
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.8)
- **OS**: Linux
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved\:
  
drm/panthor\: Fix TOCTOU race between GROUP\_REGISTERED check and erase
  
panthor\_group\_destroy\(\) checks the GROUP\_REGISTERED mark and then calls  
xa\_erase\(\) without holding the xarray lock across both\. If the handle is  
destroyed by another thread in between, and a concurrent GROUP\_CREATE  
reuses the freed ID, the first destroy erases and releases the new,  
still\-initializing group, leading to a use\-after\-free in  
panthor\_group\_create\(\)\.
  
Do the mark check and erase atomically under xa\_lock\(\)\.
