# Paymenter is a free and open\-source webshop solution for management of hosting services\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-103026
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.1)
- **言語**: PHP
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

Paymenter is a free and open\-source webshop solution for management of hosting services\. Prior to 1\.5\.7, the HasConfirmation trait in app/Livewire/Traits/HasConfirmation\.php, included by app/Livewire/Component\.php across 36 client\-facing Livewire components, exposed executeConfirmedAction as a public action that invoked any component method accepted by method\_exists\. The action could call inherited InteractsWithProperties\:\:fill\(\), whose data\_set\(\) writes arbitrary public properties, including state marked with \#\[Locked\] and dotted paths into hydrated Eloquent models\. Depending on the methods and properties exposed by an individual component, an unauthenticated caller could use this path to bypass intended authorization and modify server\-side state intended to be immutable or protected from client\-side manipulation\. This issue is fixed in version 1\.5\.7\.
