## 原文

\#\#\# Patches Fixed in \*\*4\.6\.2\*\*\. Upgrade with \`pip install \-\-upgrade mail\-parser\`\. Candidate addresses are now selected by position, not by appearance — a bracketed address the sender wrote is byte\-identical to one the MTA wrote, so no amount of pattern matching can tell them apart\: \- the first token of the \`from\` clause is the HELO name, whatever its shape, and is never a candidate; \- an explicit HELO marker inside a comment group is sender text and is excluded; \- a candidate must sit inside a \`\(\` / \`\[\` group; \- IPv4 and IPv6 matches are merged positionally, never family\-first; \- the walk stop
