- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-62337 / GHSA\-x26x\-7f4v\-cqx7

## 原文

\#\#\# Summary The Halo 2 aggregation circuit could mishandle proof elements encoded as the point at infinity\. When an assigned proof point appeared in an MSM term with constant scalar one, the Halo 2 loader used a scalar\-one shortcut that passed the assigned point directly into the EC sum helper\.
