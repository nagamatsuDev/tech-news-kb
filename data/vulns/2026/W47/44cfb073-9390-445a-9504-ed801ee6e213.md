# Dell OpenManage Enterprise, versions prior to 4\.8\.0, contains an Improper Privilege Management vulnerability\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-101213
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.8)
- **公開**: 2026-10-30

## 原文
