# In Modem, there is a possible out of bounds write due to an integer overflow\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-20548
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **OS**: Network/Firmware
- **公開**: 2026-11-02

## 原文

In Modem, there is a possible out of bounds write due to an integer overflow\. This could lead to remote escalation of privilege, if a UE has connected to a rogue base station controlled by the attacker, with no additional execution privileges needed\. User interaction is not needed for exploitation\. Patch ID\: MOLY01872867 / MOLY01774038; Issue ID\: MSV\-9801\.
