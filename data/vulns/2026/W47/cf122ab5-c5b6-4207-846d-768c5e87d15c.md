# TeamPass folder re\-parenting authorization bypass

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-103035 / GHSA\-66q9\-mxf2\-xqw6
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.3)
- **言語**: PHP
- **パッケージ**: Packagist/nilsteampassnet/TeamPass &lt; 3\.2\.2\.0 → 3\.2\.2\.0
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

\#\# Summary Missing authorization in the update\_folder action\: any authenticated non\-read\-only user can rename, re\-parent and convert to personal any folder in the instance, moving another team's credential subtree into their own personal space and removing it from the organization\. Confirmed at HEAD 2f271cdd \(3\.2\.1\.1\), and present at 3\.1\.3\.1 and 3\.1\.7\.6\. \#\# The defect The folder\-management endpoint dispatches an update\_folder action that renames and re\-parents a folder\.
