\#\#\# Summary The fix for \[AIKIDO\-2024\-10565\]\(https\://intel\.aikido\.dev/cve/AIKIDO\-2024\-10565\) \(plaintext password logging\) introduced two redaction helpers — \`DriverUtil\.toStringWithoutSensitiveValues\(\)\` and \`DriverUtil\.redactSensitiveValuesInJdbcUrl\(\)\` — but both helpers only redact the \`"password"\` property/parameter \(\`JdbcUrlUtil\.TAG\_PASSWORD\`\)\. The equally sensitive \`"token"\` property \(\`JdbcUrlUtil\.TAG\_TOKEN\`\), used for DataStax Astra and other token\-based Cassandra authentication, is completely unredacted by both helpers\.
