# Sprout Track is a self\-hosted baby tracking application\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-77338
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.3)
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

Sprout Track is a self\-hosted baby tracking application\. Prior to 1\.3\.5, Sprout Track uses a publicly known hardcoded signing secret when JWT\_SECRET is unset, and standard setup does not generate a unique secret\. On a network\-reachable affected deployment, an unauthenticated attacker can sign JWTs with arbitrary claims, including system\-administrator claims, to access administrative functionality and read or modify family, child activity, account, and email data\. Deployments with a strong unique JWT\_SECRET are not affected\. Managed and hosted instances were reported remediated\. This issue is fixed in version 1\.3\.5\.
