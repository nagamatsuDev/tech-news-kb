# Next\.js\: SVG rejection bypass in the image optimization

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-119347 / GHSA\-7qx7\-33qx\-rqx2
- **危険度**: Medium（警告）
- **言語**: JavaScript/TypeScript
- **パッケージ**: npm/next &gt;\= 15\.0\.0, &lt;\= 15\.5\.28 → 15\.5\.29, npm/next &gt;\= 16\.0\.0, &lt;\= 16\.4\.4 → 16\.4\.5
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

On self\-hosted deployments, the Next\.js image optimizer \(\`/\_next/image\`\) can be made to process an attacker\-supplied SVG even when SVG support is disabled with \`dangerouslyAllowSVG\: false\` \(the default\)\. This reaches an SVG parser the application intended to keep disabled, increasing exposure to denial of service and SVG decoder vulnerabilities\.
