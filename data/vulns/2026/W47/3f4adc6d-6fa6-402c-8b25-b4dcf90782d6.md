# The CoE SDO download handler for flexible\-length objects \(VISIBLE\_STRING, OCTET\_STRING, and similar\) does not check that the master\-decla\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-129796
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-21

## 原文

The CoE SDO download handler for flexible\-length objects \(VISIBLE\_STRING, OCTET\_STRING, and similar\) does not check that the master\-declared transfer size fits within the object before writing\. Two distinct writes are affected\: the VISIBLE\_STRING zero\-padding memset and the first\-frame copy into the object buffer\.
