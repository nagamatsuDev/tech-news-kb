- **危険度**: High（重要）

## 原文

\#\#\# Impact After receiving an HTTP redirection, Git LFS may forward certain HTTP headers to a host other than the one for which they were intended\. While \`Authorization\` headers are never forwarded to inappropriate hosts, \`Cookie\` headers may be forwarded incorrectly\. Git LFS has resolved this issue by never forwarding \`Cookie\` headers to hosts other than the ones for which they were intended\.
