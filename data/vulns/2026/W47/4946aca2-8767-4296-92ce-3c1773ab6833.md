# Mareforma is a local verification layer for AI\-assisted research\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-71536
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.0)
- **公開**: 2026-11-18

## 原文

Mareforma is a local verification layer for AI\-assisted research\. From 0\.3\.0 until 0\.3\.8, verify\_bundle in mareforma/export\_bundle\.py validates the outer DSSE envelope and exporter\-signed subject digests, but JSONLDExporter\.\_claim\_node in mareforma/exporters/jsonld\.py supplies no signatureBundle for individual claims\. Because the exporter signs both the predicate nodes and their digest list, the check proves only internal bundle consistency and allows claims with missing, invalid, or forged asserter signatures to be accepted as authentic\. This issue is fixed in version 0\.3\.8\.
