# An insufficient session expiration vulnerability in WatchGuard Dimension's administrator session management allows an attacker holding a \.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-93916
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.6)
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文
