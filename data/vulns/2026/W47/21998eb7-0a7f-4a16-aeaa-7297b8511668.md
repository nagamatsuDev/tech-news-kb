## 原文

Phoenix is an AI observability and evaluation platform\. From 13\.0\.0 until 15\.7\.0, the applyChatTemplate GraphQL query passed attacker\-controlled F\-string templates to FStringTemplateFormatter\.\_format, while DotDict\.\_\_getattr\_\_ did not prevent traversal through Python private attributes\. A crafted field could traverse object internals such as \_\_class\_\_, \_\_init\_\_, and \_\_globals\_\_ to read process environment variables and internal module state\. The endpoint is unauthenticated on default installations, and any account can reach it when authentication is enabled\. Exposure of PHOENIX\_SECRET can allow administrative session\-token forgery and decryption of credentials protected by keys derived from that secret\. This issue is fixed in version 15\.7\.0\.
