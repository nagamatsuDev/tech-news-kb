- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-128073 / GHSA\-5hj8\-q7fv\-qrj6
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)

## 原文

\#\#\# Summary An attacker who can supply a crafted SAML Response for parsing can crash a \`gosaml2\` service provider by sending an \`EncryptedAssertion\` with AES\-GCM encryption metadata and a \`CipherValue\` shorter than the GCM nonce size \(12 bytes\)\. The library panics with \`slice bounds out of range\` before any validation completes\.
