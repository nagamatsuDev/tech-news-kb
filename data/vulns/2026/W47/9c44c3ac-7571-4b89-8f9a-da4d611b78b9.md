# gosaml2 is a Pure Go implementation of SAML 2\.0\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-128073
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **公開**: 2026-11-17

## 原文

gosaml2 is a Pure Go implementation of SAML 2\.0\. Prior to 0\.12\.0, ValidateEncodedResponse\(\) calls decryptAssertions\(\) on an EncryptedAssertion before signature or content validation completes, and EncryptedAssertion\.DecryptBytes\(\) in types/encrypted\_assertion\.go slices decoded ciphertext without first validating the required nonce or initialization\-vector length\. A CipherValue shorter than the 12\-byte AES\-GCM nonce causes a slice\-bounds panic, and an empty AES\-CBC CipherValue passes the modulo block\-size check before causing a slice\-bounds panic at the 16\-byte initialization\-vector split\. An attacker needs only the service provider's public certificate to wrap a symmetric key, and no valid identity\-provider signature or relationship is required\. The unrecovered panic can terminate the request goroutine or the service process\. This issue is fixed in version 0\.12\.0\.
