# Pi\-hole FTL is the core engine of the Pi\-hole network\-level advertisement and tracker blocker\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-71304
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-17

## 原文

Pi\-hole FTL is the core engine of the Pi\-hole network\-level advertisement and tracker blocker\. From 6\.0 until 6\.7, check\_client\_auth\(\) in src/api/auth\.c detects that a matching session identifier has passed valid\_until but still assigns user\_id before the expired state can be rejected\. That assignment sends execution through the authorized branch, renews valid\_until for another timeout interval, and leaves the session\-expired response unreachable\. An attacker who possesses a previously valid session identifier can periodically reuse it to preserve administrative access indefinitely, making the configured session timeout ineffective\. This issue is fixed in version 6\.7\.
