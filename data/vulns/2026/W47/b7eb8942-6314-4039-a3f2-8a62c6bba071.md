# IBM Guardium Data Protection 12\.2 is vulnerable to insecure deserialization in the SharePoint Tap MulticastTransceiver component\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-84236
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文
