# OpenID4VP\: X\.509 chain validation accepts a forged certificate that reuses a trusted anchor's Subject DN without verifying its signature

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-75522 / GHSA\-4c2f\-96cf\-f5fc
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **言語**: JavaScript/TypeScript
- **パッケージ**: npm/@openeudi/openid4vp &lt;\= 0\.8\.0 → 0\.8\.1
- **公開**: 2026-11-18

## 原文

\#\# Summary \`ChainBuilder\` \(the private RFC 5280 validator behind \`TrustEvaluator\` and the public credential\-parsing API\) terminated chain building on \*\*Subject Distinguished Name string equality\*\* rather than on cryptographic closure to a trust anchor\. A certificate whose Subject DN string matched a trusted anchor's Subject DN was accepted as the chain terminus \*\*with no signature verification\*\* against the anchor's public key\. An attacker who mints a self\-signed certificate and copies a trusted anchor's Subject DN string onto it can therefore have that certificate — and any credential signed
