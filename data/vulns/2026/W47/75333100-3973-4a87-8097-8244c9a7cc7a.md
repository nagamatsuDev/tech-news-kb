# In Eclipse Mosquitto versions 1\.6 to 2\.0\.22 and 2\.1\.0 to 2\.1\.2, an MQTT v5\.0 client connecting with a will\-delay\-interval that subsequent\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-126763
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)

## 原文

In Eclipse Mosquitto versions 1\.6 to 2\.0\.22 and 2\.1\.0 to 2\.1\.2, an MQTT v5\.0 client connecting with a will\-delay\-interval that subsequently disconnects with a shorter interval causes all delayed wills to be suppressed\.
