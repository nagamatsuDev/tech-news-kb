# Open OnDemand is an open\-source high\-performance computing portal\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-55171
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.1)
- **公開**: 2026-11-18

## 原文

Open OnDemand is an open\-source high\-performance computing portal\. Prior to Open OnDemand 4\.1\.6 and 4\.2\.3, and prior to ood\-ansible 4\.2\.1, the portal generator defaults OIDCCryptoPassphrase to SHA1\(servername\), and the templates/ood\_portal\.yml\.j2 role checks a different variable from the documented passphrase setting\. Administrators who set ood\_oidc\_crypto\_passphrase can therefore believe a strong secret is configured while the deployment continues to use an attacker\-derivable value\. In OIDCSessionType client\-cookie deployments, an unauthenticated remote attacker can derive the encryption key and forge a mod\_auth\_openidc\_session cookie for another user, while server\-cache deployments permit lower\-impact tampering with protected OIDC state and nonce cookies\. This issue is fixed in Open OnDemand versions 4\.1\.6 and 4\.2\.3 and ood\-ansible version 4\.2\.1\.
