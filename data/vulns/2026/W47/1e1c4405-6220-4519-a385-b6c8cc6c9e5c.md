# DOMPurify SAFE\_FOR\_TEMPLATES bypass\: split template expressions survive in attached shadow root content on DOM output modes

- **ID**: GHSA\-cg2g\-xh64\-v4q7
- **危険度**: Low（注意）
- **言語**: JavaScript/TypeScript
- **パッケージ**: npm/dompurify &gt;\= 3\.4\.4, &lt;\= 3\.4\.16 → 3\.4\.17
- **公開**: 2026-10-22

## 原文

\#\# Summary When DOMPurify is configured with \`SAFE\_FOR\_TEMPLATES\: true\` together with a DOM\-returning mode \(\`RETURN\_DOM\: true\`, \`RETURN\_DOM\_FRAGMENT\: true\`, or \`IN\_PLACE\: true\`\), template expressions such as \`\{\{\.\.\.\}\}\`, \`\$\{\.\.\.\}\`, and \`&lt;%\.\.\.%&gt;\` survive sanitization inside the text content of an attached shadow root\. The post\-walk safety net \`\_scrubTemplateExpressions\` \(\`src/purify\.ts\:1717\`\) was extended to recurse into \`&lt;template&gt;\.content\`, but it never recurses into attached shadow roots\.
