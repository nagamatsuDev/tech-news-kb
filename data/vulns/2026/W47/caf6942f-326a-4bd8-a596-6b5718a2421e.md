# In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved\: ALSA\: emux\: Fix UAF of soundfont zones at note\-on snd\_emux\_note\_on\(\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-99484
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.8)
- **OS**: Linux
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved\:
  
ALSA\: emux\: Fix UAF of soundfont zones at note\-on
  
snd\_emux\_note\_on\(\) looks up the matching zones via  
snd\_soundfont\_search\_zone\(\), which takes sflist\-&gt;lock only during the  
lookup and returns the raw zone pointers after releasing it\.  The  
caller then stores them in vp\-&gt;zone without any protection\.  
Meanwhile, a concurrent SNDRV\_EMUX\_IOCTL\_REMOVE\_LAST\_SAMPLES ioctl may  
unlink and free those zones in snd\_soundfont\_remove\_unlocked\(\); this  
leads to a use\-after\-free in snd\_emux\_note\_on\(\), and later at  
accessing the cached vp\-&gt;zone, e\.g\. in snd\_emux\_send\_effect\(\)\.
  
Fix it by holding sflist\-&gt;lock in snd\_emux\_note\_on\(\) over the whole  
zone lookup and voice setup, and let snd\_soundfont\_search\_zone\(\)  
require the caller to hold the lock, instead\.  
snd\_soundfont\_lock\_preset\(\) takes sflist\-&gt;lock for setting  
presets\_locked, so the removal path now syncs with the note\-on finish\.
