## 原文

OpenCTI is an open source platform for managing cyber threat intelligence knowledge and observables\. Prior to 7\.260624\.0, an Organization Administrator with VIRTUAL\_ORGANIZATION\_ADMIN and KNOWLEDGE\_KNUPDATE can use organizationFieldPatch to replace the administered organization's grantable\_groups without a SETTINGS\_SETACCESSES authorization check\. The administrator can add the built\-in Administrators group to that grant scope and then bypass the grantable\_groups guard added by the incomplete CVE\-2026\-44730 remediation to add their own account to Administrators\. Membership grants the BYPASS capability after session cache refresh, escalating the tenant\-scoped account to global administrator with access across organizations, users, settings, markings, connectors, and ingestion\. This issue is fixed in version 7\.260624\.0\.
