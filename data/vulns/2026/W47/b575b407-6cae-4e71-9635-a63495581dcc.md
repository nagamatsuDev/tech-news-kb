# Mitel MiCollab Weak Authentication Vulnerability

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-133311
- **危険度**: Medium（警告） (CVSS 5.9) / 🔥 悪用確認 (CISA KEV)
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

A vulnerability in the Audio, Web, and Video Conferencing \(AWV\) component of Mitel MiCollab through 10\.3 \(10\.3\.0\.18\) could allow an unauthenticated attacker, with knowledge of a specific system\-generated random identifier or pre\-staged content, to conduct an unauthorized access attack due to improper authentication mechanisms\. A successful exploit could allow an attacker to obtain sensitive information\. The vulnerability is limited to the affected functionality and does not provide unrestricted access to arbitrary files elsewhere on the system\. This is MTLVULN\-1863\.
