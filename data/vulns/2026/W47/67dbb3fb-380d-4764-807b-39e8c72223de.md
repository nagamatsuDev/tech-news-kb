# A malicious actor with access to the network and under certain conditions could exploit an Improper Certificate Validation vulnerability \.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-132807
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.0)
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文
