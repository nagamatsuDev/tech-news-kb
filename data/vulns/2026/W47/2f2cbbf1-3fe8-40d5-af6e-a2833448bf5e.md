# The PDO mapping bit lengths stored in the mapping objects are writable by the master via SDO\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-129811
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-21

## 原文

The PDO mapping bit lengths stored in the mapping objects are writable by the master via SDO\. sizeOfPDO summed them without checking the total against the size of the rxpdo\[\] or txpdo\[\] process data buffers\. A master can craft a mapping whose total bit count exceeds those buffers, causing COE\_pdoPack and COE\_pdoUnpack to write past their ends during each process data cycle after the PREOP to SAFEOP transition\.
