# In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved\: ALSA\: line6\: Reject capture buffers smaller than two packets line6\_\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-99611
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.8)
- **OS**: Linux
- **公開**: 2026-11-20

## 原文

In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved\:
  
ALSA\: line6\: Reject capture buffers smaller than two packets
  
line6\_capture\_copy\(\) splits the copy into the ALSA ring buffer once at  
the buffer end and wraps pos\_done once, which is only correct when one  
packet holds at most buffer\_size frames\. The packet size comes from the  
device's endpoint, and the per\-device hw structs allow 64\-byte periods  
with periods\_min 1, so a packet can be larger than the whole buffer\.  
audio\_in\_callback\(\) logs an oversized packet and then copies it anyway\.
  
The packet size is known at probe time, so constrain  
SNDRV\_PCM\_HW\_PARAM\_BUFFER\_BYTES in snd\_line6\_capture\_open\(\) to at least  
twice the larger of the two endpoints' packet sizes\. Two rather than  
one, because pos\_done advances by the packet and wraps once, so a buffer  
of exactly one packet leaves the position unchanged after every packet\.  
hw\_params then rejects a buffer that cannot hold two packets, and the  
single wrap is valid for every configuration that gets through\. Keep a  
guard in line6\_capture\_copy\(\) that drops an oversized packet with a  
ratelimited error, for the case that should no longer be reachable\.
  
Reproduced on 7\.3\-rc7 with a USB/IP device presenting the GuitarPort  
interface layout and 1024\-byte packets into a 128\-byte buffer\: ALSA  
logged "invalid position\: pos \= 224, buffer size \= 32" and the driver  
logged "driver bug\: len" on every following packet\. With this patch a  
1024\-byte buffer is refused at hw\_params, and a 2048\-byte buffer streams  
for 30 s with no report and with the position moving inside the buffer\.
