- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.8)
- **OS**: macOS, iOS
- **公開**: 2026-11-21

## 原文

An integer overflow was addressed with improved input validation\. This issue is fixed in iOS 27\.2 and iPadOS 27\.2, macOS Golden Gate 27\.2, macOS Sequoia 15\.8\.2, macOS Tahoe 26\.7\.2, tvOS 27\.2, visionOS 27\.2\. Processing a maliciously crafted USD file may lead to unexpected app termination\.
