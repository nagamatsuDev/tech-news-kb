# netty\-incubator\-codec\-ohttp\: OHTTP codecs retain consumed outer cumulation buffers after completed exchanges

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-69133 / GHSA\-cg26\-4c6j\-2x8h
- **危険度**: Medium（警告） (CVSS 5.3)
- **言語**: Java/Kotlin
- **パッケージ**: Maven/io\.netty\.incubator\:netty\-incubator\-codec\-ohttp &gt;\= 0\.0\.20\.Final, &lt;\= 0\.0\.26\.Final → 0\.0\.27\.Final
- **公開**: 2026-11-20

## 原文

\#\# Summary\: OHttpClientCodec and OHttpServerCodec retain the encrypted outer HttpContent in a handler\-level cumulationBuffer\. After a valid OHTTP exchange completes, the OHTTP request/response context is destroyed, but the outer cumulationBuffer is not released or reset\. It remains referenced by the codec until a later inbound content frame replaces it or the handler is removed/channel is closed\. This can pin attacker\-influenced pooled/direct buffer capacity on idle keep\-alive channels\. Across many long\-lived idle connections, this can create availability pressure\. Affected components\: \* codec
