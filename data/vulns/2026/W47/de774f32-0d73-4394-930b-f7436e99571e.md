# An out\-of\-bounds read was addressed with improved bounds checking\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-112465
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.5)
- **OS**: macOS, iOS
- **公開**: 2026-11-21

## 原文

An out\-of\-bounds read was addressed with improved bounds checking\. This issue is fixed in iOS 27\.2 and iPadOS 27\.2, macOS Golden Gate 27\.2, tvOS 27\.2, visionOS 27\.2\. An app may be able to cause unexpected system termination or read kernel memory\.
