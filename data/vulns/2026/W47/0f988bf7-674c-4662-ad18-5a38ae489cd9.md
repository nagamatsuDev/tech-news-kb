# Mitel MiCollab Not Failing Securely Vulnerability

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-133324
- **危険度**: Medium（警告） (CVSS 5.3) / 🔥 悪用確認 (CISA KEV)
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

A vulnerability in the Audio, Web, and Video Conferencing \(AWV\) component of Mitel MiCollab through 10\.3 \(10\.3\.0\.18\) could allow an unauthenticated attacker to conduct an authentication bypass attack due to improper handling of authentication\. A successful exploit could allow an attacker to bypass authentication and perform unauthorized actions within the context of the application\. This is MTLVULN\-1877\.
