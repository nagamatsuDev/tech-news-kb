# IBM Guardium Data Protection 12\.2 is vulnerable to local privilege escalation via the SUID\-root podman\_wrapper binary on the Collector ap\.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-84087
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 7.8)
- **公開**: 2026-11-19

## 原文

IBM Guardium Data Protection 12\.2 is vulnerable to local privilege escalation via the SUID\-root podman\_wrapper binary on the Collector appliance\. A local attacker with low\-privileged access to the Collector can exploit insufficient argument validation in the SUID binary to execute arbitrary commands as root, resulting in full compromise of the Collector appliance\.
