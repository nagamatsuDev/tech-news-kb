# The QUIC stack queues a RETIRE\_CONNECTION\_ID frame also for retransmitted NEW\_CONNECTION\_ID frames\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-107494
- **危険度**: Critical（緊急） (CVSS 9.1)
- **公開**: 2026-11-18

## 原文

The QUIC stack queues a RETIRE\_CONNECTION\_ID frame also for retransmitted NEW\_CONNECTION\_ID frames\. This allows a remote malicious QUIC stack to queue an unlimited number of RETIRE\_CONNECTION\_ID frames\.
