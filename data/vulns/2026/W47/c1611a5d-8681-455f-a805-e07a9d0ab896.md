# In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved\: smb\: client\: fix slab\-out\-of\-bounds write in id\_mode\_to\_cifs\_acl\(\) \.\.\.

- **ID**: CVE\-2026\-99292
- **危険度**: High（重要） (CVSS 8.8)
- **OS**: Linux
- **公開**: 2026-11-16

## 原文

In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved\:
  
smb\: client\: fix slab\-out\-of\-bounds write in id\_mode\_to\_cifs\_acl\(\)
  
In id\_mode\_to\_cifs\_acl\(\), while the chown branch reserves  
sizeof\(struct smb\_ntsd\) \+ 2 \* sizeof\(struct smb\_sid\) \(plus  
sizeof\(struct smb\_acl\) and the worst\-case ACE space after  
commit 0ee150794c75 \("smb\: client\: fix heap overflow in DACL owner/group  
rewrite"\)\), the chmod branch only adds 2 \* sizeof\(struct smb\_ace\) or  
5 \* sizeof\(struct smb\_ace\) to secdesclen \(or 1 \* sizeof\(struct smb\_ace\)  
on posix\)\.
  
However, the chmod branch also invokes build\_sec\_desc\(\) \-&gt;  
copy\_sec\_desc\(\), which unconditionally writes the Owner SID at  
pnntsd \+ sidsoffset and the Group SID at  
pnntsd \+ sidsoffset \+ sizeof\(struct smb\_sid\) \(2 \* 68 \= 136 bytes total\),  
and set\_chmod\_dacl\(\) always writes a struct smb\_acl header \(8 bytes\)  
even when the source descriptor has dacloffset \=\= 0\. When a security  
descriptor has compact or overlapping Owner/Group SIDs \(or  
dacloffset \=\= 0\), calling chmod\(\) under\-allocates pnntsd and writes past  
the end of the slab buffer\.
  
Reserve sizeof\(struct smb\_acl\) \+ 2 \* sizeof\(struct smb\_sid\) \(plus  
sizeof\(struct smb\_ntsd\) on the posix path\) in the chmod branch of  
id\_mode\_to\_cifs\_acl\(\)\.
